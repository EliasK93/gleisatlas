# Map, station detail and search

## 1 Overview

The start page of the app: a map of Germany with one dot per station, coloured by the selected metric, a panel for the clicked station, a detail page per station and a global search for stations and lines. Visual targets are in [design.md](design.md) §2.1 and §2.2 and the clickable [mockup](design/mockup.html); where they differ, the mockup wins. The frame around these pages (sidebar, filter bar, mobile bars) is described in [app-shell.md](app-shell.md), the metrics and the API of the statistics in [patterns.md](patterns.md), the coordinate data in [pipeline.md](pipeline.md) and [adr/0001-station-coordinates.md](adr/0001-station-coordinates.md).

## 2 Spec

**Routes and URL keys**

| Path | Page |
|---|---|
| `/karte` | Map start page; `/` and unknown paths redirect here, query string kept |
| `/bahnhof/{eva}` | Station detail page |
| `/lage` | Live-Lage, the map for one day (see [day-history.md](day-history.md)) |

The filter keys `g`, `von`, `bis` and `k` are the ones of the shell (see [app-shell.md](app-shell.md)). On the map, `von` and `bis` may be months or days (the Karte is the one page with a day-level Zeitraum); the station page, like the other month pages, rounds days outward to months. On the map, `bhf` is the selected station: it opens the panel, so a link or a reload restores the selection. The detail page takes the station from the path and drops a `bhf` or `linie` key from the URL once; the map drops `linie` too. A station picked from the search goes to `/bahnhof/{eva}` and keeps `g`, `von`, `bis` and `k`. From the detail page, the breadcrumb "Karte" and "Groß ansehen →" (the heatmap) carry the filters plus `bhf={eva}`. Every URL write puts the keys in one canonical order, so a filter change never reloads the page.

**API used**

- `GET /karte` (`gattung`, `von`, `bis`) returns every station with coordinates and its four statistics in one response; the metric switch is client-side. `von` and `bis` are each a month (`YYYY-MM`: as `von` its first day, as `bis` its last day) or a day (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Without `von` the range starts on the first day of `vollabdeckung_ab` (unless `bis` ends before it), without `bis` it runs to the last day in the data. It reads the day table, so the response's `von` and `bis` are the first and last day with data in the selection. `ohne_koordinaten` counts named stations with stops in the selection that have no coordinates.
- `GET /stationen/{eva}/statistik` (same filters) feeds the detail page: overall statistics, the delay distribution (7 bins plus cancelled) and the statistics per category group.
- `GET /heatmap?eva=` feeds the mini heatmap on the detail page and shares the cache with the heatmap page.
- `GET /suche?q=&limit=` feeds the search (stations and lines, see **Search** below). `GET /stationen?q=&limit=` feeds the station chip's combobox and is the station half of `/suche`: name-prefix matches first, then by number of stops, so Köln Hbf comes before Köln-Dellbrück.
- `GET /stationen/{eva}` resolves a `bhf` that is not in the map data (a station without coordinates).
- `GET /stationen/{eva}/linien` (same filters) feeds the table of lines; `GET /zeitverlauf?eva=` (category only) feeds the sparkline, together with `/datenstand` for the gap hours.

**Map page**

- Full-bleed map (MapLibre GL, OpenFreeMap "positron" style in the light theme and "dark" in the dark one, see [theme-and-language.md](theme-and-language.md)), no footer. Labels of the base map appear from zoom 7, so the dots dominate the overview.
- Floating card top left: h1, lead text, metric switch, filter chips. The Zeitraum chip holds two native date fields, "Zeitraum von" and "Zeitraum bis", limited to `erster_tag` to `letzter_tag` of `/datenstand`; without `von` and `bis` in the URL they show the first day of the full-coverage month and the last complete day. A day outside the data, or a month beyond it, shows the nearest day inside. A pick that would cross the other edge moves that edge to the same day; typing a day key by key into a field passes through such days, so a day that would cross is held while the field has the focus and applied when the field is left. A field writes only a complete day inside the data. Legend bottom left (collapsed on mobile) with the metric's name (its explanation stands under the metric switch), the five classes, the "sehr wenig Daten (n < 15)" (very pale ring), "wenig Daten (n < 50)" (pale dot) and size keys and the meta line "period · category · Bahnhöfe auf der Karte: x" (the period as days, for example "1.–29. Okt. 2026"), followed by " · ohne Koordinaten: y" when stations with data have no coordinates. Zoom buttons bottom right, the attribution below them; from 768 px it wraps before it reaches the legend.
- On desktop the start view fits Germany below the filter card (its measured bottom edge plus 12 px, fitted again while the card's height settles and until the camera is moved or a station is picked); with the station panel open the card stops 12 px before it and its rows wrap.
- One circle per station: radius from the square root of its stops, fill from the class of the displayed value. A station below the low-data limit is hollow with a ring in the scale colour, at 30 % opacity; a station with a "~" value (15 to 49 counted stops) is filled at 50 % opacity; a station whose stops were all cancelled uses the cancellation colour. Dots with enough data draw above the faint ones, and within each group worse classes draw on top. The selected station gets an accent ring.
- Hover (on devices that hover) shows "name · value"; a click selects the station. A pick through the chip or a link eases the camera to the station, clear of the panel; a click on the map does not move the camera.
- Station panel: 360 px at the right on desktop, a bottom sheet of at most 62 % height on mobile. It shows name, EVA, a little-data note, one sentence ("x von 10 Ankünften sind pünktlich, 9 von 10 höchstens y min spät"), four tiles, the meta line, "Alle Details" and "Verbindung ab hier" (tagged "bald"). Schließen and Escape close it and hand the focus to the map.
- **Lines in the panel (the Karte only).** After the two buttons: the heading "Häufigste Linien" with the name of the metric at its right and the months below it on one line of their own (for example "Okt. 2026"), then the station's five busiest lines by stops. A row has the train-type badge, the line name and the value in the map's metric, grey when the data is thin (the rule of `show()`); it links to the line page with `k`, `von` and `bis` but not the category ([lines.md](lines.md)). When the station has more than five lines, "Alle {count} Linien" leads to the station page at its table of lines (`#linien`, the page scrolls there once the lines have loaded). The lines come from `GET /stationen/{eva}/linien` with the map's category and months, the request the station page makes, so that page opens from the cache. The months are the ones the lines cover: a day range on the Karte rounds outward to whole months, no range means the full-coverage start to the newest month. States: a skeleton while loading, the error banner with "Erneut versuchen", and "Mit diesen Filtern gibt es hier keine Linien." Rows are at least 44 px high on a phone. Live-Lage's panel has no lines.
- A `bhf` without map data (unknown or no coordinates) shows the panel with "EVA …" as title and a note, plus the real name when `/stationen/{eva}` knows it.
- States: on the first load a card centred over the map with a spinner and "Lade Bahnhöfe …", after 5 s with a line that counts the elapsed seconds, "Der Server startet gerade (7 s), das kann bis zu einer Minute dauern." (a cold start of the deployed API takes up to about 40 s; Karte and Live-Lage, and the page skeletons of the other pages carry the same hint; re-check: the loading-card tests in `frontend/e2e/map.spec.ts` and `frontend/e2e/lage.spec.ts`), empty card with "Filter zurücksetzen", error banner with "Erneut versuchen" that keeps the filters.

**One day**

- The Karte shows a period of days (`von`, `bis`), not one day: it has no Tag switch and ignores a `tag` key. Live-Lage in turn ignores the Karte's `g`, `von` and `bis` and leaves them in the URL ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md) §2). The map of one day against its normal is the separate page Live-Lage (`/lage`), which shares this page's map code; its rules, the steps and the data are in [day-history.md](day-history.md) §2 and §4.

**Station detail page**

- Header: breadcrumb "Karte › name", h1, "EVA … · federal state", "Teilen"; "Verbindung ab hier" and "Umstiege hier" are tagged "bald". Filter bar without the Bahnhof chip.
- A little-data banner, four tiles (Pünktlichkeit, Typische Verspätung, Schlechteste 10 %, Ausfälle) each with value, sentence and meta line, the delay distribution with a separate hatched "ausgefallen" bar, the mini heatmap with "Groß ansehen →", bars for Regionalverkehr and Fernverkehr, the table "Linien an diesem Bahnhof" and the card "Letzte 12 Monate".
- "Linien an diesem Bahnhof" lists the lines that stop here, worst punctuality first by default, with a segmented toggle "Schlechteste zuerst" · "Beste zuerst" (client-side: only the lines with enough data are reversed, the ones with too little data stay last in both orders), with the train-type badge, the name, a thin bar with the punctuality value, n and the cancellation rate as its own grey "✕" figure. It shows the first ten and "Alle {count} Linien anzeigen" expands the rest in place and the same button then reads "Weniger anzeigen" and collapses back to ten. A line with too little data is hatched and shows no value, by the rule of the heatmap. Every row links to the page of that line ([lines.md](lines.md)), with the selected metric and the period but not the category. After "Alle {count} Linien anzeigen" the focus moves to the first newly shown row; after "Weniger anzeigen" it stays on the button. On mobile each row becomes a bordered card with the bar below the name. It has its own loading, error and empty state ("Mit diesen Filtern gibt es hier keine Linien."), so the rest of the page stays. The card is the anchor `#linien`: a link with that hash (the panel's "Alle {count} Linien") scrolls to it once the lines have loaded, and the page's URL cleaning keeps the hash.
- "Letzte 12 Monate" is a sparkline of the punctuality of the station's last 12 months with the latest value and its n ("pünktlich im {month} · n = …", the latest month with enough data, or "–" when no month has) and the link "Zeitverlauf →" to the station's time series. It follows the category filter but not `von` and `bis`, which the series does not use. Months with little data (the rule of the heatmap) or gaps are dashed, with a hollow point, and a tap or hover shows the month's tooltip. It has its own loading, error and empty state.
- The metric switch above the mini heatmap drives the mini heatmap and the category bars and writes `k`.
- States: skeleton, empty period with "Filter zurücksetzen", error banner, and "Diesen Bahnhof kennen wir nicht" with a search for an unknown or invalid EVA (an invalid EVA does not ask the API).
- Mobile: one column, tiles 2 × 2.

**Search**

- A combobox "Suche" over `/suche` with the placeholder "Bahnhof, Linie …" ("Station or line …"): in the sidebar under the logo on desktop with the hint "Strg K", inline in the mobile top bar. Ctrl+K and Cmd+K focus the visible field; there is no palette dialog. It finds stations and lines.
- The results are one listbox "Suche" with up to 8 options per group. Each group has a small visible heading ("Bahnhöfe", "Linien") and the groups stand in the order of `zuerst`; a group without hits is not shown. With no hit at all the text reads "Kein Bahnhof und keine Linie gefunden".
- **Stations:** name and "federal state · EVA … · n Halte" (missing parts left out). A name that appears more than once among the shown stations gets "2 Einträge mit diesem Namen".
- **Lines:** the train-type badge (when the type is known), the label and, when the query named an end station, a second row "Richtung {ziel}". The order is the API's, so prefix hits follow the exact ones.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys run through all options in the order shown, across the groups; Enter and click pick; Escape clears. Enter is ignored while the answer for a new term loads. Option ids are the field's id plus the option's index, because a line id holds a colon and spaces, which `aria-activedescendant` cannot reference.
- **A station pick** goes to `/bahnhof/{eva}` and keeps `g`, `von`, `bis` and `k`. **A line pick** goes to `/linie/{id}` (percent-encoded) and keeps `k`, `von` and `bis` but not the category or the station, plus `richtung=2` when the hit carries direction 2: "ic34 dortmund" opens `/linie/IC34%3A08000046?richtung=2`, while a line toward its last station opens without a `richtung` key. The keys are written in the canonical order, so the line page does not redirect.
- The station chip of the filter bar uses the same combobox in its station-only scope: `/stationen`, no group headings, and it sets `bhf` instead of navigating. The Zeitverlauf's Linie chip uses it in its lines-only scope ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md)).

**Search rules** (implemented in `core/search.py`; this is the one place they are written down)

- **Normalisation.** Case, spaces and hyphens do not matter: "S 6", "s6" and "S6" are one query, "ic-34" reads like "IC34". The query is compared with the line names written the same way.
- **Lines are found by name only**, never by an end station alone. The line part is the longest leading run of words whose joined text is a line name exactly ("ice 679", "s 6"), else the longest run that is the start of one ("re5" also finds RE50). A query without such a run ("dortmund", "köln hbf") is a place: no line is listed, however many lines end there.
- **End-station words narrow a line.** The words after the line part must all occur in one end station of the line's route (the first or the last station of its axis) or in its label, else the id drops out. "ic34 dortmund" keeps the ids of IC34 with an end at Dortmund; "s6 köln" finds S6 Essen Hbf – Köln-Worringen, whose label names Köln although its axis ends at Düsseldorf Hbf (the label names the two most frequent end stations, which differ from the axis's ends for about half of the lines). After an exact line name only the ids of that name stay: "re1 hamm" lists RE1 Hamm – Aachen, not RE13 or RE190.
- **The word picks the direction, from the axis's ends only.** When the end station is the axis's first station the hit carries `richtung` 2 (the line page's direction toward it), when it is the last `richtung` 1, and `ziel` names that station; with the words at both ends, in the label only, or without end-station words, both are null and the page opens in its default direction.
- **Order inside the lines:** exact name before prefix, lines before single trains ("ICE 679" is a train), more stops over the whole data set first, then the label. One name has many ids (S6 runs in about ten networks), so every id is its own hit, with its label naming the end stations.
- **Who leads.** Lines lead when the query starts with an exact line name, at least one line was found and no listed station's name starts with the query (compared in lower case): "s6", "S 41", "ice 679", "ic34 dortmund". In every other case the stations lead, also for a bare prefix of line names or a place. The station condition is there because the source has junk line names such as "Leer", "E" and "RE", which are also the start of station names: without it "leer" would list a line above Leer (Ostfriesl), and typing "Essen" or "Regensburg" would flip the groups after the first letters. The stations are the `/stationen` ranking, prefix matches before the busiest.

**`GET /suche`**

- Query: `q` (1 to 100 characters), `limit` (1 to 20, default 8, applied to each group); 422 otherwise.
- Returns `bahnhoefe` (as `/stationen`: `eva`, `name`, `land`, `n`), `linien` (`linie`, `label`, `typ`, `zug`, `richtung`, `ziel`) and `zuerst` (`bahnhoefe` or `linien`, the group to list first).
- The frame of all line ids (about 19,000: about 1,250 lines and the rest single trains) is built once and kept until one of `linien.parquet`, `lines.parquet`, `linienweg.parquet` or `stations.parquet` changes (path and modification time are the cache key), so new data is never served stale and no new data file is needed. Measured on the real data: the first call about 230 ms (it builds the frame), later calls about 21 to 28 ms, part of that the station ranking.

## 3 Decisions

- **Station coordinates from Wikidata.** Reason: product owner's choice after weighing five sources; 99.9 % of all stops belong to stations with coordinates (see the ADR).
- **MapLibre GL with the OpenFreeMap "positron" style (light) and "dark" (dark theme, [theme-and-language.md](theme-and-language.md)).** Reason: product owner's choice; zoom, pan and draw order over about 5,000 points need WebGL, and OpenFreeMap needs no API key. The no-library alternative is an SVG outline map, which the product owner declined.
- **Base map labels follow the UI language** (`name:de`, else the local `name`); the OpenFreeMap styles prefer English names ("Cologne"). `localLabel` in `metrics.ts` rewrites every label that reads a name; road numbers stay. Reason: a German UI with English place names reads wrong, and the English UI gets English labels from the same code.
- **Coordinates live in the gitignored `data/` folder**, built by `pipeline.coords`, not committed. Reason: they are derived data like the raw files, and the result depends on the current state of Wikidata.
- **The selected station lives in `bhf`.** Reason: the panel survives a reload and the heatmap link carries the station without a second key.
- **The mockup wins over design.md** where they differ: the sheet is fixed at 62 %, there is no `z` map-position key, the footer is hidden on the map and the metric switch scrolls sideways on mobile. Reason: the mockup is the clickable reference.
- **Search per mockup, no palette dialog.** Reason: product owner's choice; Ctrl+K only focuses the field. It now finds lines as well, which answers the question whether it should grow into a palette: stations and lines come from the same field, pages stay in the navigation.
- **One global search for stations and lines.** Reason: lines were reachable only through a station's line table or the Rankings; the sidebar field is where people start. The main navigation stays as it is; restructuring it is open (roadmap, "Ideas, unsorted").
- **A place lists stations, not the lines that end there.** "dortmund" shows the Dortmund stations, not the many lines that end at Dortmund Hbf. Reason: the product owner's choice; a place is nearly always a station query, and the lines at a station belong on its page.
- **A line name plus an end station opens that line toward that station.** "ic34 dortmund" puts IC34 first and the pick opens `/linie/IC34%3A08000046?richtung=2`. Reason: the product owner's choice; one line name has many ids and two directions, and the end station is what tells a person which is meant. Rest words match the end stations or the label only, so a station on the way does not find the line (see Known shortcuts).
- **Spaces, hyphens and case are ignored**: "S 6" is "S6". Reason: the product owner's choice; line names are stored without the inner space ("S6", "IC34") while people type both ways, and train numbers keep their space ("ICE 679") but are found the same way.
- **Prefix hits follow the exact ones, and lines come before single trains.** "re5" lists RE5, then RE50, then the train RE 501. Reason: a person typing a name wants that name first, and a line says more than one train number.
- **No filter field in the Rankings.** Reason: the global search covers finding a line by name.
- **The field's accessible name is "Suche"; the filter chip's combobox stays "Bahnhof suchen".** Reason: two comboboxes with one name would break tests that find the chip by name, and accessibility labels are not changed to make tests easier. The field was "Bahnhofssuche" while it found stations only.
- **The placeholder is "Bahnhof, Linie …" ("Station or line …"), not "… suchen …".** Reason: the sidebar is 248 px wide at every desktop size, so the field always has the same 125 px of text room: 32 px for the magnifier on the left, 64 px on the right for the "Strg K" hint (55 px) and a gap. "Bahnhof oder Linie suchen …" measured 193 px and "Bahnhof oder Linie …" 141 px, which ran under the hint; "Bahnhof, Linie …" measures 110 px, the English one 107 px. The magnifier already says "search". The fit test ends the room at the hint's left edge and measures both languages in the sidebar and the phone bar; `text-ellipsis` cuts a wider fallback font before the hint. Alternatives that were not taken: hiding the hint, or a smaller font.
- **Option ids are index-based.** Reason: line ids contain ":" and spaces, which `aria-activedescendant` cannot reference.
- **Only the global search names its groups.** The single-kind pickers list their options without headings. Reason: a list of one kind needs no heading, and the heading space matters in a small popover.
- **The search is one request, `/suche`, with the order decided by the server (`zuerst`).** Reason: the rules for who leads depend on the line names, which the browser does not have, and one round trip serves both groups.
- **The search normalises with lower case, not case folding.** Reason: the same function runs in Python and, on the columns, in Polars' `to_lowercase`; case folding would differ for "ß".
- **Unbuilt actions** ("Verbindung ab hier", "Umstiege hier") are shown with a "bald" tag, not hidden. Reason: product owner's choice; the detail page builds what the data supports and says what is missing.
- **"Linien an diesem Bahnhof" shows punctuality only, worst first, and does not follow the metric switch.** The first ten rows show, the rest expands in place. Reason: the API orders lines by punctuality, and a table that re-sorted with the metric would need a second ordering rule; sorting by column is not built (see Known shortcuts).
- **The panel lists the five busiest lines, not the worst.** Reason: the busiest are the lines people know at that station, and the panel is a first look; the worst first order stays on the station page, which has all of them.
- **The panel's lines are on the Karte only.** Reason: line numbers are monthly. For single days only `fahrten.parquet` has lines, and only for the last 28 complete days and never for today, so Live-Lage would show lines for some days and none for others.
- **The lines come after the buttons.** Reason: on a phone the sheet is 62 % high, and "Alle Details", the main action, must stay visible without scrolling; the five rows and their link would push it below the fold when placed above.
- **The months stand below the heading, on one line of their own.** Reason for naming them: the Karte may show days, the lines are whole months, and two periods side by side without a label would contradict each other; the station page rounds the same way. Below the heading because beside it a long range broke over two lines (the product owner's pick).
- **A line row opens the page of the line.** Reason: a line has a page of its own ([lines.md](lines.md)), and its Zeitverlauf is one click further, from the page's sparkline. An earlier version opened the series directly.
- **"Letzte 12 Monate" is punctuality over the last 12 months of the data, not a calendar year.** The series is the time series endpoint's, so the sparkline and the full page show the same numbers. Reason: one source for both, and the period picker stays out of it like on the full series.
- **No list alternative to the map.** Reason: search and panel reach every station without the map. The rankings start with ten rows and grow by "Mehr anzeigen" up to 2,000, but they filter by a minimum sample, so they are no full station list either.
- **The duplicate-name hint is computed in the browser** from the shown results, not by the backend. Reason: names are not unique, but a backend field for one hint is not worth the contract.
- **Delay bins are half-open in whole minutes**: < 1 (early arrivals included), [1, 3), [3, 6), [6, 10), [10, 20), [20, 30), ≥ 30. Reason: the source stores whole minutes, and the edge at 6 matches the punctuality rule. Cancelled stops are counted apart and never fall into a bin.
- **The distribution shows shares of all planned stops**, with cancellations as their own bar. Reason: the mockup does it, and the bars then add up to 100 %.
- **"x von 10" is rounded down**, so 96 % reads "9 von 10". Reason: "10 von 10" promises a certainty the number does not support.
- **Low data is judged the way the heatmap does**: arrived stops for the delay metrics, all stops for Ausfälle, "~" below 50. Reason: one rule on the map, in the panel, in the tiles and in the mini heatmap.
- **Stations with few stops are drawn pale, and beneath the others**: 50 % opacity for the "~" tier, 30 % for the hollow ring, `PALE` and `VERY_PALE` in `MapPage.tsx`. Reason: the same cut-off as the "~" everywhere (`show()`); the class from a few stops is noisy and should not draw the eye, and a pale dot must never cover a solid one. Both tiers stay visible on the light and the dark base map.
- **A metric switch on the detail page.** Reason: the mini heatmap and the category bars would otherwise be fixed to punctuality; it costs one shared component and gives `k` the same meaning as on the other pages.
- **Escape closes the panel and the focus moves to the map.** Reason: keyboard users would otherwise lose their place when the panel disappears.
- **Search hits of an old term are not opened.** Enter is ignored while the answer for a new term loads. Reason: pasting a new name over an old one must not open the old one.
- **Touch targets are 44 px** on the search field, the zoom buttons and the attribution toggle, without changing how they look. Reason: design.md §5.
- **The Karte's Zeitraum is day-level and runs to the last complete day; the other pages keep months.** Reason: the map lagged up to a month behind, and `days.parquet` already holds every station per day. The Heatmap needs the hour of the stop, which the day table lacks, and the lines have no day table; per-day tables for them would be much larger and not fit the deploy.
- **Two native date fields, not presets or a calendar.** Reason: `<input type="date">` needs no library, keeps the 44 px hit areas and takes a typed day; `min` and `max` are the first and last day of the data.
- **The default range is the first day of the full-coverage month up to the last complete day.** Reason: the same start as the month pages (a nationwide map must not mix two station populations, [patterns.md](patterns.md) §3), and the map shows the newest complete data without a pick.
- **The month pages round days from the URL outward to months and leave the URL alone** (the rule, `useMonthPeriod` and the reason are in [app-shell.md](app-shell.md) §2 and §3).
- **`/karte` reads `days.parquet`, the station rankings and statistics stay on `aggregate.parquet`.** Reason: the map needs days, the rankings and the station page months, and the aggregate carries the hour and weekday that the heatmap needs; one table cannot serve both without a bigger file.
- **`/karte` is cached per process** by filter combination and file modification times. Reason: the first computation takes seconds (on one Polars thread, measured on the real data: October 2026 0.57 s, the default range 2.7 s), later calls about 50 ms; the cache follows the files, so new data is never served stale.
- **GZip on all responses, at level 5.** Reason: the all-stations response is about 900 KB as JSON and about 230 KB compressed. Starlette's default level 9 took 23 of the 33 ms of a warm `/karte` locally, level 5 takes 9 ms for a slightly larger body (0.26 MB compressed at level 9); on the 1 vCPU container compression had been about two thirds of the CPU time of the most used request.
- **The state of a station is its code, and the UI names it through the catalogs.** `Station.land` in `/stationen`, `/stationen/{eva}` and `/suche` carries the code (`nw`, `hb`, as `/tageskarte` does, from `core.stations.LAND_CODES`), the station page and the search hits print `land.<code>`, and a station whose Wikidata label is no German state has none. Reason: the raw Wikidata label was printed as it came, so the English UI said "Nordrhein-Westfalen" next to Live-Lage's "North Rhine-Westphalia", and Bremen read "Freie Hansestadt Bremen" here and "Bremen" there; one code and one catalog keep the wording in one place.
- **The panel's lines and the station page's mini heatmap are keyed by station.** `PanelLines` is rendered with `key={eva}`, and the mini heatmap uses the heatmap page's query key (the line slot empty), so "Groß ansehen" sends no second `/heatmap` request. Reason: with the previous answer kept under the new station's name the panel showed Köln's lines under Hamburg Hbf until its own answer arrived, and the two keys differed by one slot, so the cache was never shared.
- **The worker is bundled** (`maplibre-gl-worker.mjs?worker&url`). Reason: MapLibre v6 otherwise looks for it relative to its own module, which breaks once bundled.
- **The MapLibre container sits in a positioned wrapper.** Reason: MapLibre's stylesheet sets its container to `position: relative`, which collapsed a container that was meant to be `absolute`.

## 4 Architecture and data keys

- `frontend/src/MapPage.tsx`: the page (also Live-Lage, through its `day` prop), `StationMap` (MapLibre lifecycle, one GeoJSON source with a circle layer and a selection ring, features built from the theme tokens at runtime) and `Legend`.
- `frontend/src/mapBase.ts`: what both maps share (the Karte and the line page's `LineMap`): MapLibre's worker and CSS, `STYLE` per theme, `baseMap` (the map with its controls and attribution), `useBaseStyle` (the effect that reloads the style on a theme switch and marks the container `data-ready="false"` until the map is idle) and `themeColours` and `token`, which read the theme tokens.
- `frontend/src/StationPanel.tsx`: `StationPanel` (the side panel and the phone's bottom sheet) and `PanelLines` (the five busiest lines, `PANEL_LINES`).
- `frontend/src/StationPage.tsx`: the detail page with its tiles, distribution, mini heatmap, category bars, the `Lines` table and states. The `Sparkline` card is `ZeitverlaufPage.tsx`'s export (see [app-shell.md](app-shell.md)).
- `frontend/src/StationSearch.tsx`: `StationSearch` (the combobox, variants "global" and "inline", ids from `useId`; the prop `scope` is `alle` (stations and lines from `/suche`, with group headings), `bahnhoefe` (the default: `/stationen`) or `linien` (the lines group of `/suche`); `onPick(eva)` for a station, `onLine(line)` for a line), `useOpenStation`, `GlobalSearch` (scope `alle`, opens a line with `richtung` where the hit has one), `useSearchShortcut`. `frontend/src/Filters.tsx`: `Picker`, the popover around the inline combobox for the Bahnhof chip and the Zeitverlauf's Linie chip.
- `frontend/src/ui.tsx`: pieces shared by the pages: metric switch, error banner, empty card, share button, "bald" tag, the low-data rule `show()` (a second implementation of the API's `shown`, see day-history.md §8), the class fills, and `Badge`, `MiniBar` and `Cancelled` for the lines table and the category bars.
- `frontend/src/metrics.ts`: also `share`, `binLabel`, `tenths`. `frontend/src/urlKeys.ts`: the URL keys (`readFilters`, `writeFilters`, `readRank`, `writeRank`, `MIN_NS`, `lineSearch`, `linePageSearch`); `withParam` writes keys in canonical order.
- `backend/main.py`: `/karte`, `/stationen/{eva}/statistik`, the shared filter chain `selection`, the cache `karte_for`, the query type `Period` (a month or a real day; only `/karte` takes it, a calendar-impossible day such as `2026-02-30` is a 422). Also `/suche` with `find_stations` (shared with `/stationen`) and the cached line frame `_search_lines`.
- `core/search.py`: `norm`, `split_query` and `line_hits` (the hits and whether the query starts with an exact line name), pure functions over a line frame (`linie`, `name`, `label`, `typ`, `zug`, `n`, `first`, `last`) and its normalised names, which `_search_lines` caches with it.
- `frontend/src/Filters.tsx`: the Zeitraum chip with `DayField` (the date field shared with Live-Lage). `frontend/src/metrics.ts`: `monthOf`, `monthsOf`, `firstDay`, `lastDay`, `formatDays`; `withPeriod` is in `frontend/src/urlKeys.ts`. `frontend/src/api.ts`: `useMonthPeriod`, `useStation` (the station lookup the panel and the Bahnhof chip share) and `useStationLines` (the lines of a station for a category and the months; the panel's `PanelLines` in `StationPanel.tsx` and the station page's `Lines` use the one query key `["linien", eva, g, von, bis]`). `useHashScroll` in `frontend/src/ui.tsx` scrolls to the URL hash once the data that sizes the page is in.
- `core/stats.py`: `delay_bins`. `core/stations.py`: `ibnr_expr` (EVA to IBNR).
- `pipeline/coords.py` and `pipeline/aggregate.py`: coordinates and the station table, see [pipeline.md](pipeline.md).
- Data keys: `data/stations.parquet` carries `eva`, `name`, `n`, `lat`, `lon`, `land`; `/karte` reads `data/days.parquet` (`day`, `gruppe`, `eva`, `bucket`, `n`; see [pipeline.md](pipeline.md)), filtered by `day` so that Polars can skip row groups; `bhf` and `k` as in the URL keys above.
- Tests: `frontend/e2e/map.spec.ts`, `station.spec.ts` and `search.spec.ts` run against fixtures served through `page.route`. The OpenFreeMap style is answered by a minimal local style, never the network; the page's `data-ready` marker is set after the map is idle with the data drawn. The `/suche` stand-in answers each term the specs type with a canned line list and lead; its stations are the `/stationen` stand-in. The search rules themselves are tested in `tests/core/test_search.py` and `tests/api/test_search_api.py`.

## 8 Known shortcuts

- MapLibre is in the main bundle: the main script is about 1.5 MB (about 400 kB gzip, not re-measured) plus a worker of about 520 kB. Lazy-load the map page if the first load of other pages matters (`ponytail:` in `App.tsx`).
- A metric switch rebuilds and re-sends all points; move to feature state if it stutters (`ponytail:` in `MapPage.tsx`).
- The bottom sheet has a fixed height, without drag or snap levels (`ponytail:` in `StationPanel.tsx`).
- The term explanations on the detail page are native `title` tooltips, which are hover-only; use a popover for touch (`ponytail:` in `MetricTiles`, `ui.tsx`, shared with the line page).
- The search sends one request per keystroke, cached per term; add a debounce if the API gets slow (`ponytail:` in `StationSearch.tsx`).
- `/karte` is cached per process, with at most 16 selections; memory and the first computation grow with the number of days in the range (`ponytail:` in `backend/main.py`). The statistics are already vectorised (`cells_of`), which measured no gain on a year of days, since the scan dominates; a slower `/karte` needs a smaller scan.
- `/karte` with only an early `bis` (no `von`) labels its range from the first day in the file (2024-06-30), a day before `erster_tag`, because the full-coverage default is switched off for it (`ponytail:` in `period_start`). Only hand-made URLs reach it; the date fields never write one.
- Stations without coordinates are not on the map but remain in search and detail. On the months to 2026-09 that was only Trebitz (b Könnern), with one stop and no Wikidata item; a missing station gets its IBNR added to its Wikidata item, so the next `pipeline.coords` run places it. Secondary EVAs placed by the same-name fallback sit under their main station's dot. The federal state is missing for some stations that have coordinates.
- The table of lines is not sortable by column. Lines with too little data (the hatched ones) come after all others, so the list shows them but never leads with them. The list is not cached (about 0.1 s for Köln Hbf, see [patterns.md](patterns.md)).
- Only an end station of a line or its label narrows a search ("ic34 dortmund"); a station on the way, or "the lines that stop at X", does not find a line. That needs a per-station line list in the search and is a roadmap item under "Ideas, unsorted".
- The duplicate-name hint only knows the eight results shown (`ponytail:` in `StationSearch.tsx`).
- The mini heatmap is a colour grid with one accessible label; the full heatmap is the accessible table. The category bars are scaled to the larger category for all metrics except punctuality.
- MapLibre's compact attribution starts expanded until the first map move; on mobile it covers a strip above the legend until then.
- Vite starts on its port strictly and fails if the port is taken, instead of moving to a port CORS would block (the CORS rule is in [patterns.md](patterns.md) §4).
